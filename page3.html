Discover Honduran Food

La Ceiba has delicious local food and traditional Honduran dishes for visitors to enjoy.

Baleadas

Baleadas are a popular Honduran food made with a flour tortilla, beans, and other fillings.

Seafood

Visitors can look for seafood dishes and enjoy the flavors of Honduras's Caribbean coast.

Local Restaurants

Explore local restaurants to discover Honduran food and new favorite meals.
